## Amendment

This constitution is changed by an ordinary spec that is `approved`, claims the
affected heading as a section unit of this file, and contradicts no
`specs/000` `unamendable` anchor.

`amends` is **not** the instrument: its targets are spec ids, and this file is
not a spec. The claim uses the ordinary ownership vocabulary over a section
unit: `establishes` for a principle the spec adds, `refines` (with a named
`aspect`) for one it tightens, `co_authority` for one genuinely shared. The
anchor is the heading slug.
